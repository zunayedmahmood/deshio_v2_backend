Deshio Barcode Mapping Test

Login, then run the automated barcode mapping test. It creates a diagnostic PO in store_id=2, receives it through the real PO receiving code, scans every generated barcode through POS and lookup logic, logs mismatches/errors, then deletes the PO through the existing PO deletion route.

Target setup: 1000 barcodes with 5 units per product. This requires 200 product variants, so the backend starts with 10 random SKU groups and keeps filling from additional SKU groups until the barcode target is reachable.
